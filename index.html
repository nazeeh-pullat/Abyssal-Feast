0 m The game

A reef that fights back

Abyssal Feast is a fast, colourful underwater survival game. Swim with one thumb, eat anything smaller than you, and watch out for everything bigger.

Every level teaches the reef something new: pufferfish that bounce you away, anemones that sting, clams that snap shut, currents that sweep you off course and bosses that hunt you down. Grow through four size stages, chain your bites into a Feast Streak, and unlock new fish, hats, glasses and necklaces along the way.

It is built to be played in short bursts or long sessions - with a full Chapter 1 to conquer today and four more chapters of the ocean still to come.

The Feast Streak

Bite again within 3 seconds and your streak spells out another letter: FEAST, then FEAST RUSH, FEAST SURGE, FEASTSTORM, GRAND FEAST, SAVAGE FEAST - all the way to ABYSSAL FEAST. Reach it and the game roars the name - then it starts spelling again.
