Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to the Android game Abyssal Feast and this website

The short version
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The only time the game talks to our server is when you type a redeem code.
  • We never sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

1. Who we are

Abyssal Feast is made by Nazeeh Pullat, an individual developer publishing as NP Games ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the limited information described below. Contact: nazeehfazal12@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (shells, black pearls, owned fish and accessories, unlocked levels, achievements, scores) and your settings (volume, graphics, difficulty) in a save file in the game's private storage. This file is not sent to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game deletes it.

The game also creates a random device ID (a made-up string, not your advertising ID, phone number or hardware serial). It is stored on your device and is only used for redeem codes (see section 3).

3. Redeem codes - the one thing that goes online

When you enter a redeem code, the game sends the code and your random device ID to our small server (a Cloudflare Worker with a Cloudflare D1 database) so it can check the code is valid and that it has not been used up or already used by your device.

DataWhyHow long
The code you enteredTo check it is valid and give the rewardRedemption records are kept for as long as the code exists, to stop double use
Random device ID + time of redemptionTo make sure one device uses a code only onceSame as above
IP address (as a salted one-way hash, never the raw address)To rate-limit people guessing codesAbout 24 hours, then deleted

Cloudflare, which hosts the server, necessarily processes your IP address to deliver the request, under its own privacy terms. We do not see your name, email, location or contacts, and we do not combine this data with anything else. If you never enter a redeem code, nothing is sent.

The game does not otherwise connect to the internet.

4. Device permissions (all optional)

  • Your own pictures: in Custom mode you can add your own fish pictures. The game uses the system photo picker, so it never gets access to your photo library or a storage permission. The picture you choose is copied into the game's private storage and stays on your device.
  • Your own music (Android): if you choose to play your own music (Settings → Your Music) the game asks permission to read audio files so you can pick one. The file is used only on your device. You can play without granting it.
  • Vibration: the game can vibrate your device. You can switch this off in Settings.

5. Purchases and ads

The game currently has no real-money purchases and no advertisements. If they are added later, they will be handled by your app store and/or a named advertising network, and this policy will be updated, listing what those services collect, before that version is released.

6. This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no cookies, no analytics, no trackers and no forms, and its fonts and images are served from the site itself (not from Google or other third parties). As with any web server, GitHub may log technical request data such as your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement. If you choose to email us, we use your address only to reply.

Links on the site may take you to services such as Google Play, YouTube or Discord, which have their own privacy policies.

7. Third parties

Your app store (Google Play) has its own privacy policy and may collect information when you install or update the game. We do not control that. The only service providers that handle data for us are Cloudflare (redeem server) and GitHub (this website).

8. Children

Abyssal Feast is a general-audience game intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Traffic to the redeem server is encrypted (HTTPS). IP addresses are stored only as salted hashes. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the data we hold to the minimum needed.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data we hold about you, to object to its processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We do not sell or share personal data for advertising. The only data we could link to you is the random device ID and the code you redeemed; email us with the code and we will look it up, and delete the record on request. Everything else lives on your device: clear the app's data or uninstall to remove it.

11. Where data is processed

Cloudflare operates a global network, so redeem requests may be processed in countries other than your own. Cloudflare provides contractual safeguards for international transfers.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at this address. Material changes (such as adding ads or analytics) will also be shown in the game.

13. Contact
